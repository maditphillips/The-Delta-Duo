HOW BIG IS A MATCHUP? — other ways to measure it
Regular season 2012-2025, weeks 5+. Same players and over/under as FINDINGS.txt.
Matchup metric = BOARD.txt metric: RB scrimmage yds allowed, WR / QB fantasy pts allowed;
opponent-adjusted, blended with last season as 8 games. Everything is PPR.
boom = scored 1.5x his baseline or more;  bust = half his baseline or less.
