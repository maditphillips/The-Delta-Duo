DEFENSIVE MATCHUP METRICS — which defensive number best predicts a fantasy matchup?
nflverse play-by-play, weekly stats and closing lines; regular season 2012-2025
Forecast sample: weeks 5+ using only games played before kickoff.

Target   = player PPR points minus his average in his other games that season
           (RB/WR: >=7 PPR baseline, touched the ball; QB: the listed starter, >=10 baseline).
r        = mean of 14 single-season Pearson correlations; ±95% across seasons.
           Positive = the metric points the right way (bigger number, softer matchup);
           metrics marked (-) are "higher = tougher" so they should come out negative.
r²%      = share of the week-to-week over/under the metric explains.
soft-tough = PPR pts/game vs the softest quarter of defenses that week minus the toughest quarter.
stab     = odd-week vs even-week reliability of the defensive number itself (season de-meaned).
