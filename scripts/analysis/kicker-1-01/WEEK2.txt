WHAT THIS MODEL RANKS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
====================================================================================================
  It ranks EXPECTED MAKE RATE on one kick. It says nothing about how
  many kicks a man gets, and that is where the rest of the
  disagreement with intuition lives. Volume - team quality, coach
  aggression, red-zone efficiency, the short leash - is a separate
  term and belongs in a separate term.
