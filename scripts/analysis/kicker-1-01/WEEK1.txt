  kicker-season pairs with 20+ attempts each, 2010-2025:
    raw FG%                            r = +0.044

  percentage points, while a 28-attempt season carries 6.8 points of

====================================================================================================
WHAT THIS MODEL RANKS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
====================================================================================================
  It ranks EXPECTED MAKE RATE on one kick. It says nothing about how
  many kicks a man gets, and that is where the rest of the
  disagreement with intuition lives.

  Note which way shrinkage cuts: it moves a POOR kicker UP, because
  his badness was mostly noise and shrinking pulls him toward average.
  If the ranking should punish a kicker his coach does not trust, that
  belongs in a volume term - expected attempts - not in the accuracy
  term. Volume is the honest home for team quality, coach aggression
  and the short-leash effect.
