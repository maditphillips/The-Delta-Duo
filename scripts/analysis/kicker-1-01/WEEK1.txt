====================================================================================================
WHY THE TWO DIFFER
====================================================================================================
  Both inputs are anchored on the same league average, so averaging
  them averages two copies of it. Take a kicker 3 points below
  average at a venue 2 points below average:
    as specified : 0.75 x (L-0.02) + 0.25 x (L-0.03) = L - 0.0225
    on deviations: L + (-0.02) + 0.25 x (-0.03)      = L - 0.0275
  The first understates it, because averaging pulls the venue
  penalty toward the kicker's number instead of adding to it. The
  75/25 split was expressing confidence in each estimate, and
  confidence belongs on the kicker's DEVIATION - shrink the noisy
  term toward zero - not on the level.
