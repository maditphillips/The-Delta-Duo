==============================================================================
everything above compares him to the average NFL leg, because a team
can sign an average leg off the street. The quarterback comparison in
PICK.txt is against replacement level, so here is the same baseline.

  replacement = kicker-seasons of 5-20 attempts (82 of them),
  the mid-season signings a team actually reaches for:
    league average, all attempts     : 0.547 points left per attempt
    full-season starters (20+ att)    : 0.521

  a replacement leg leaves 1.35x what an average one does.
  channel 1 against replacement: 24.1 points a season
  A total against replacement  : 40.6 points = 1.14 wins a season
  (channel 3 is left alone: the fourth downs he unlocks are unlocked
   by the guarantee, not by the leg he replaced.)

==============================================================================
10. SENSITIVITY AND WHAT IS NOT IN HERE
==============================================================================

-- blocked kicks, which the guarantee arguably should not cover
  blocked attempts inside 60: 144 (1.7% of attempts)
  they carry 2.2 of channel 1's 17.8 points a season
  treating a block as a miss he cannot prevent: channel 1 becomes 15.6, A becomes 31.4 points = 0.88 wins

-- where you draw the line: 55, 60 and 65 yards
  guaranteed to 55 yards: channel 1  16.0, channel 3   4.0, A total  22.1 points = 0.62 wins
  guaranteed to 60 yards: channel 1  17.8, channel 3  13.7, A total  33.7 points = 0.94 wins
  guaranteed to 65 yards: channel 1  18.3, channel 3  27.3, A total  47.8 points = 1.34 wins

-- effects left out, and which way they push
  pushing the number UP:
    - the offence would play differently on first through third down.
      Third and 15 at your own 45 is a checkdown instead of a heave.
      Not modelled at all.
    - the going-for-it baseline is selection-biased upward, because
      coaches go for it when they like their chances. Every fourth
      down where he beats a go-for-it is therefore understated.
    - the end-of-half channel, 1.5 points a season, is left out.
  pushing the number DOWN:
    - the opponent adapts. Punt coverage, two-minute defence and
      fourth-down choices all change once the other side knows the 42
      is a scoring position. None of that is in a frozen-history study.
    - blocks are credited to him above; see the first check here.
    - expected points count garbage-time points at full value. The
      competitive-snaps-only check in section 6 puts that at roughly
      a quarter of the total.
