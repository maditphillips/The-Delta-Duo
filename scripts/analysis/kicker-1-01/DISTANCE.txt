3. DOES 'KICK THE MOMENT YOU CROSS THE 50' WORK AT 70 YARDS?
==============================================================================
At 60 yards this idea lost 4.2 wins a season (EARLY.txt). The longer
guarantee starts the range further out, where a drive is worth less,
so the trade should be less bad. Expected points added by kicking on
this snap instead of running the play:

yl    (0, 20]  (20, 32]  (32, 42]  (42, 47]  (47, 52]
down                                                 
1.0     -3.48     -2.38     -1.64     -1.17     -0.87
2.0     -3.11     -2.01     -1.20     -0.76     -0.48
3.0     -2.47     -1.44     -0.52     -0.05      0.24
4.0     -1.68     -0.45      0.75      1.21      1.51

  Still negative on every down except fourth. It is less bad out at
  the 47-52, but 'less bad' is not 'good': first down there is worth
  -0.87. The answer does not change - the value is on FOURTH down.

  60-yard guarantee: 5.46 drives a game reach the 42, kicking on arrival costs -1.63 a drive
    = -151 points a season = -4.22 wins

  70-yard guarantee: 6.51 drives a game reach the 52, kicking on arrival costs -1.07 a drive
    = -118 points a season = -3.31 wins

==============================================================================
4. THE CATCH
==============================================================================
  Two things get shakier at 70 that were solid at 60.

  1. Nobody has ever done this. The longest made field goal in the
     data is 68 yards. Across 1999-2025 there are 6 attempts of
     70 or more, 0 of them made, and 43 attempts of 64 to 69,
     8 made. Every number above prices a play that does not exist,
     using the value of the field position it replaces. That part is
     solid; what is untested is whether the snap, hold and protection
     even hold up at that range.

  2. The opponent adapts harder. At 60 the guarantee touches a
     narrow strip of field. At 70 it covers everything past your own
     48, which changes punt coverage, two-minute defence and
     fourth-down policy for the other team on more than half the
     field. A frozen-history study cannot see any of that, and it all
     cuts against the kicker.
