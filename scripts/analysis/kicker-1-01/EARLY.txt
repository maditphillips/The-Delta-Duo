3. BUT WHAT IF THE DEFENCE IS GREAT?
==============================================================================
The argument has a real kernel: guaranteed threes are low variance, and
a good defence in a low-scoring game might prefer certainty to upside.
The problem is the size of the toll. Check whether teams with elite
defences actually lose less from the trade:

          drives  pts_allowed_pg  cost_per_drive
def_tier                                        
elite       6619           19.04           -1.63
good        6240           21.59           -1.63
poor        5949           23.94           -1.63
worst       5526           27.17           -1.60

  The toll is the same whoever is on defence, because it is paid on
  YOUR side of the ball: you are throwing away your own drive. A good
  defence makes each point you hold more valuable, it does not make
  giving up a point and a half per drive cheaper.

4. WOULD IT HAVE PUT HOUSTON IN THE SUPER BOWL?
==============================================================================

  HOU 2024: 110 drives reached the 42 (6.47 a game over 17 games)
    those drives actually scored      : 361 points (21.2 a game)
    the policy would score            : 330 points (19.4 a game)
    raw scoreboard change             : -31 points
    but expected points added         : -194, i.e. -5.42 wins WORSE
    and the opponent gets +35 extra possessions from drives that would otherwise have ended
    in a punt or a turnover deep in their own territory

  HOU 2025: 113 drives reached the 42 (6.65 a game over 17 games)
    those drives actually scored      : 380 points (22.4 a game)
    the policy would score            : 339 points (19.9 a game)
    raw scoreboard change             : -41 points
    but expected points added         : -204, i.e. -5.69 wins WORSE
    and the opponent gets +28 extra possessions from drives that would otherwise have ended
    in a punt or a turnover deep in their own territory

  Houston scores FEWER points in both seasons on the raw scoreboard,
  before counting the possessions handed back early. The idea does not
  need the sophisticated accounting to fail; it fails on points.
