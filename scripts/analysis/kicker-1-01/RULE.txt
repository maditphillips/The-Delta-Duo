2. THE RULE: KICK ON FOURTH AND N OR LONGER, INSIDE THE 42
==============================================================================
  'conv% at N' is the fourth-down conversion rate at exactly N to go,
  inside the 42: the threshold the rule is implicitly using.
  'bad_kicks%' is the share of the extra kicks where kicking was worse
  than the play the team actually called - the rule's own mistakes,
  and they are charged against the total.
  'wins_avg_leg' is the same rule run with an ordinary NFL kicker, so
  the gap between the last two columns is the guarantee itself.

-- read it plainly
  kick on 4th & 3 or longer (a 51% conversion or worse):
    0.51 extra attempts a game (9 a season), 2.43 total
    9.7 points a season from the new kicks, 29.7 all in
    0.83 wins - against 0.53 for the same rule with a normal leg
  kick on 4th & 5 or longer (a 42% conversion or worse):
    0.35 extra attempts a game (6 a season), 2.27 total
    9.6 points a season from the new kicks, 29.5 all in
    0.83 wins - against 0.59 for the same rule with a normal leg
  kick on 4th & 7 or longer (a 37% conversion or worse):
    0.25 extra attempts a game (4 a season), 2.17 total
    8.0 points a season from the new kicks, 27.9 all in
    0.78 wins - against 0.60 for the same rule with a normal leg

  the money is between the 33 and the 42 - a 51 to 60 yard kick.
  That band alone is 9.2 of the 9.7 points, on 0.27 attempts a game,
  and 54% of those snaps were punts. Inside the 25 the rule actively costs points:
  a guaranteed 3 is worth less than a shot at 7 when you are already
  expected to score.

-- the cost of keeping it simple
  best simple rule (4th & 4+)        : 0.83 wins
  deciding every fourth down on expected points: 0.94 wins
  the gap is the 15% of rule-driven kicks that were
  the wrong call - mostly short fields where a touchdown was live.

-- one extra clause fixes most of that: don't kick from inside the 25
  4th & 4+, any yard line   : 0.42 extra att/game,   9.7 pts, 0.83 wins (15% bad kicks)
  4th & 4+, outside the 20  : 0.35 extra att/game,  10.1 pts, 0.84 wins (5% bad kicks)
  4th & 4+, outside the 25  : 0.33 extra att/game,  10.0 pts, 0.84 wins (2% bad kicks)
  4th & 4+, outside the 30  : 0.30 extra att/game,   9.6 pts, 0.83 wins (1% bad kicks)
  4th & 4+, outside the 33  : 0.27 extra att/game,   9.2 pts, 0.81 wins (0% bad kicks)

3. WHERE THAT LEAVES THE 1.01
==============================================================================
  the rule that maximises his value is 4th & 4+: 0.83 wins
  the first overall pick has returned +1.28 wins a season on average, +1.59 median

  to match the mean: 46 points a season, so 25.8 from new kicks
    no rule gets there. The most any threshold produces is 9.7 points,
    at 4th & 4+, and pushing further makes it worse, not better.

  to match the median: 57 points a season, so 36.9 from new kicks
    no rule gets there. The most any threshold produces is 9.7 points,
    at 4th & 4+, and pushing further makes it worse, not better.

  and every row above assumes the coach follows the rule every time.
  Real coaches handed an elite leg add +0.11 attempts
  a game (OPPORTUNITY.txt). At that much extra kicking he is worth about
  0.67 wins, the 4th & 11+ row.
