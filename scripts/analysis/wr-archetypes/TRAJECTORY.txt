2. THE SAME THING AS A COEFFICIENT
==============================================================================
Holding this season's scoring fixed, what does the change from last
season add?  A positive coefficient would mean rising is good.

    Current scoring and the year-over-year change:  (n = 1,137)
      ppg                    +4.110 ppg/sd   t = +30.38   p = 0.000
      d_ppg                  -0.634 ppg/sd   t = -4.35   p = 0.000

    The same information stated as the level he came from:  (n = 1,137)
      ppg                    +3.381 ppg/sd   t = +19.77   p = 0.000
      prev_ppg_raw           +0.749 ppg/sd   t = +4.35   p = 0.000

    Those are the same regression. Given where he is now, a HIGHER
    previous season predicts a better next year, which is the same
    statement as: at equal current scoring, falling beats rising.

    Career-best season instead of last season:  (n = 1,137)
      ppg                    +3.932 ppg/sd   t = +25.69   p = 0.000
      peak_prior_ppg         -0.141 ppg/sd   t = -0.88   p = 0.379

    With age and experience in, in case 'rising' is just 'young':  (n = 1,137)
      ppg                    +4.251 ppg/sd   t = +31.68   p = 0.000
      d_ppg                  -1.032 ppg/sd   t = -6.78   p = 0.000
      age                    -1.184 ppg/sd   t = -2.66   p = 0.008
      exp                    -0.120 ppg/sd   t = -0.27   p = 0.788
