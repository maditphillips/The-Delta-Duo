THE SAME QUESTION AS A SINGLE NUMBER
========================================================================================================
Best single threshold per metric: the cut that maximises the breakout rate
above it, requiring at least 30 players on the high side.

READ THESE LIFTS AS AN UPPER BOUND. Twelve candidate cuts are searched per
metric and the best one kept, so the winning lift is optimistically biased --
the same search on noise would return something above 1.0. A lift near 1.0
after that search means the metric has no usable threshold at all. A single
cut also cannot express a middle-band effect, which is what the RB Week 1
snap-share bins look like above (55-65% best, 65%+ worse).
