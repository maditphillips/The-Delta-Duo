## 2. READING THE INTERACTION
   Rank is coded so a HIGHER number is a LATER pick. A POSITIVE log rank x
   week 1 term means Week 1 carries MORE weight for later picks; negative means
   more for early picks; near zero with a large p means the data cannot
   separate them. The marginal-slope line is the same thing in readable form.


## 3. IS THE TOP-TIER RESULT REAL, OR JUST THIN?
   The two rank measures disagree most in the top-5 tier, so both the effect
   and the sample behind it are shown side by side.
   pos  measure             n   gap ppg      95% interval     dR2

   WR   preseason ECR      28     +0.06    [-2.57, +2.69]   0.030
   WR   prior finish      119     +1.03    [-0.40, +2.45]   0.038
   TE   preseason ECR      27     +2.27    [-0.49, +5.04]   0.004
   TE   prior finish      116     +1.63    [+0.34, +2.91]   0.002
   QB   preseason ECR      30     -0.00    [-2.61, +2.60]   0.001
   QB   prior finish      118     +2.44    [+1.04, +3.84]   0.144

   Prior-season finish is not the market's view: a back who finished top-5
   last year but turns 30 behind a new line is in its top tier and nowhere
   near the top of a real draft board. That tier therefore holds uncertainty
   an actual ADP had already priced out, which is why Week 1 looks more useful
   there. Where the two measures disagree, prefer the ECR rows.
