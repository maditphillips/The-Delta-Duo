## 3. THE ANALYST'S CLAIM, TESTED DIRECTLY
   'Week 1 stars turn into duds.' They do -- but compared with whom?

   pos                  week 1 top 12         everyone else   ratio
               n  bust rate   per szn       n     bust rate
   ----------------------------------------------------------------
   QB        325        49%       5.9     668           76%    0.65
   RB        324        30%       3.6    2523           83%    0.36
   WR        327        31%       3.8    3246           77%    0.41
   TE        324        53%       6.4    1410           88%    0.60

   'per szn' is how many nameable Week 1 top-12 busts a season produces at
   that position -- the raw material for the take. The ratio is what the take
   leaves out: below 1.00 means a Week 1 star busts LESS often than the field.
