# NFL mental-performance and mental-health staff, all 32 teams (2020–2026)

Compiled 2026-11-25. This extends `sports_psychologists.md` (2026-11-22). It is an evidence table, not a classification:
you decide which teams are "confirmed has / confirmed none / unsure".

**How to read the sources**
- **Read** means the page was opened and the quote checked. Only si.com and thebanner.com pages could be opened.
- **Snippet** means only a search-result summary was seen. Team sites, ESPN, NFL.com, Fox, Boston.com and others are blocked
  here, so quotes marked Snippet are paraphrases from the search summary. Check them before you publish.
- **Clinician vs performance coach:** the league-mandated "team clinician" is a licensed mental-health provider (psychiatrist,
  doctoral psychologist, or master's-level counselor/social worker). Several clinicians are also sport psychologists who do
  performance work, so a "team psychologist" title does not settle which kind of role it is. These cases are marked **(ambiguous)**.
- **NFL Total Wellness clinician list caveat:** the league's page https://totalwellness.nfl.com/who-we-are, and an older
  dev-site copy https://nfl-totalwellness-dev-umbraco.azurewebsites.net/about-us, list a clinician for each club. The search
  summaries of these pages are scrambled. Across different searches, the same page was said to pair Chris Carr with the
  Texans, Lani Lawrence with the Jets and Carla Suber with the Bengals, and all three are wrong. Pairings taken only from
  those pages are marked **(TW pairing, unreliable)**. Don't rely on them without a second source.
- The search budget ran out near the end (200 searches). Seahawks/ESPN and Steelers/UPMC follow-ups could not be run.
