## League-wide counts (definitions and dates differ)
- 2021: Commanders' hire was "the fourth full-time clinician in psychology" in the NFL (SI, read).
- 2022: "seven NFL teams with full-time psychologists" (Post-Gazette, snippet) https://www.pressreader.com/usa/pittsburgh-post-gazette/20221023/281878712300887
- Undated: "only eight teams have made this a full time position" (Chris Carr, Packers podcast, snippet).
- March 2026: owners reportedly approved a full-time mental health clinician for all 32 teams (NSCA, single source, snippet)
